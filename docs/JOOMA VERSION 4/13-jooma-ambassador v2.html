JOOMA LTD — AMBASSADOR AGREEMENT

This agreement is between Jooma Ltd (company number to be inserted), registered in England and Wales, and Priya Raman of 42 Hartley Road, Leeds LS6 2JT ("the Ambassador").

1. What this is

You agree to promote Jooma using a unique referral code we give you. We agree to pay you commission on the subscriptions that result, and to give you a Pro account at no charge for the length of this agreement. You are not an employee, a worker, or an agent of Jooma. You are self-employed and responsible for your own tax and National Insurance.

2. How long it lasts

Twelve months from the date we activate your account. It does not renew automatically. We will write to you a month before it ends to ask whether you want to continue.

Either of us may end it earlier with 30 days' written notice. We may end it immediately if clause 6 is broken.

3. What we pay you

For each new paying subscriber who uses your code:

  • Standard, paid monthly — £1.50 for each month that subscriber keeps paying
  • Pro, paid monthly — £2.00 for each month that subscriber keeps paying
  • Max, paid monthly — £4.00 for each month that subscriber keeps paying
  • Standard, paid yearly — £18.00, once, on their first payment
  • Pro, paid yearly — £24.00, once, on their first payment
  • Max, paid yearly — £48.00, once, on their first payment

Commission is earned when a payment clears, not when someone signs up. A three-day free trial earns nothing until the first real payment is taken.

Recurring commission continues for as long as that subscriber keeps paying, including after this agreement ends, up to a maximum of 24 months from their sign-up date.

4. When we pay you

On the 15th of each month, for the month before, into the account you gave us. If the 15th is a weekend or bank holiday, the next working day.

If a month comes to less than £25.00, it rolls into the next month until the balance passes £25.00. Any balance is paid out in full when this agreement ends, whatever the amount.

5. Refunds, chargebacks and cancelled trials

If a subscriber is refunded, charges back, or cancels within our refund window, the commission for that payment is removed from your balance. If it has already been paid to you, it is deducted from your next statement.

6. What you must not do

  • Use your own code, or ask anyone to sign up on your behalf and reimburse them
  • Bid on "Jooma" or close variations in paid search
  • Offer cashback, rebates or incentives for using your code without asking us first
  • Say anything about Jooma that is not true, including about results, pricing, or data
  • Present yourself as an employee of Jooma, or speak on our behalf
  • Share pupil data, or anything that could identify a child, in any content featuring Jooma

Breaking any of these ends this agreement immediately and forfeits any unpaid balance.

7. Disclosure

You must make clear that you are paid when you promote Jooma. "#ad", "paid partnership", or similar, on every post, in line with the CAP Code and ASA guidance. This is your legal obligation as well as ours.

8. Your free Pro account

Provided for the length of this agreement, for your own teaching use. It is not transferable. It reverts to a normal paid plan when this agreement ends, and we will tell you before that happens.

9. Your data

We hold your details to run this programme and to pay you, and we keep payment records for six years as HMRC requires. Our privacy notice at jooma.ai/privacy applies. You can ask for a copy of what we hold at any time.

10. Changes to the rates

We may change the commission rates with 60 days' written notice. Subscribers who signed up before the change keep the old rate for as long as they keep paying. If you do not accept a change, you may end this agreement and we will pay your balance in full.

11. Law

This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
