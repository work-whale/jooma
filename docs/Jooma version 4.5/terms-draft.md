# Terms, section 7: draft for Jooma 4.5

Draft only. `app/terms/page.tsx` is unchanged until you approve this (and, if you wish, your solicitor has seen it). Three paragraphs change; the fourth (GBP, VAT, refunds) stays as it is.

## Paragraph 1: what is offered

**Now**

> Jooma is offered through paid monthly subscription plans, at the pricing displayed on our website at the time of purchase. Creating resources requires an active subscription or free trial, except for a small number of free tries on our website, which need no account, and the free tries offered to an account with no subscription, whose results cannot be exported.

**Proposed**

> Jooma is offered through a free plan and through paid plans billed monthly or yearly, at the pricing displayed on our website at the time of purchase. The free plan gives a set number of resources each week, and files exported on it carry a Jooma mark. Paid plans give a set number of resources each month, and paid plans may buy top ups. A small number of free tries on our website need no account.

**Why:** there is now a real Free plan, so "requires an active subscription or free trial" is no longer true; yearly billing and top ups exist; the "no subscription" free tries are retired.

## Paragraph 2: the free trial

**Now**

> A new account gets a 3 day free trial. We do not ask for payment details to start it, and nothing is charged during or after it unless you choose a plan. If you choose a plan, your subscription starts and the plan's fee is charged on that day, and any time left on your free trial ends. The free trial is available once per account.

**Proposed**

> A new account gets a free trial of the Pro plan for 10 days, ending at 23:59 UK time on the tenth day after signing up. We do not ask for payment details to start it, and nothing is charged during or after it unless you choose a plan. When the trial ends, your account moves to the free plan. If you choose a plan during the trial, your subscription starts and the plan's fee is charged on that day, and any time left on your free trial ends. The free trial is available once per email address.

**Why:** 10 days of Pro instead of 3 days; the account lands on Free afterwards; the code allows one trial per email address ever (it survives deleting the account), which is stricter than "once per account".

## Paragraph 3: renewal and cancelling

**Now**

> Subscriptions renew automatically each month unless cancelled before the renewal date. You can cancel at any time from your account, and you keep your plan until the end of the period already paid for.

**Proposed**

> Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period, monthly or yearly, unless cancelled before the renewal date. You can cancel at any time from your account. You keep your plan until the end of the period already paid for, and your account then moves to the free plan. Unused resources do not carry over from one period to the next; resources from top ups do not expire.

**Why:** yearly plans renew yearly; cancelling now lands on Free rather than a locked account; the carry-over rules are new and worth stating once.
