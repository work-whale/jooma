Refunds and cancellations

If Jooma is not right for you, we give your money back.

This page says exactly when, without the hedging. We would rather hand back a subscription than have you feel stuck with one, and we would rather you told us why than quietly resented us.

  • Applies to individual teacher subscriptions
  • Last updated 25 November 2026
  • Schools and trusts see clause 8

The short version

Five rules cover almost every situation. The clauses below only add detail.

  • Fourteen days on any payment, no reason needed Monthly or yearly. Write to us within 14 days of a charge and we refund it.
  • A renewal you did not mean to pay comes back in full Up to 30 days, as long as you have not used the plan since that payment. Forgetting to cancel is not a thing we charge you for.
  • If the fault is ours, you are refunded whatever the date Charged twice, charged on a cancelled plan, or Jooma was not working. No time limit on that.
  • Anything you made is yours to keep A refund does not take your lessons away. Unspent top ups are refundable, spent ones are not.
  • You can try Jooma for 10 days before paying anything Ten days of Pro, 300 resources, no card. Most people never need this page.

Clause 1

Your legal rights come first

Nothing on this page takes away a right you have by law. If this policy and the law disagree, the law wins and we will apply it without being argued into it.

If you are a consumer buying from us online in the UK, that includes a 14 day right to change your mind under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, and the right to a repeat performance or a price reduction if what we supplied was not of satisfactory quality, not fit for purpose, or not as described, under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

If you buy through the Apple App Store, Apple takes the payment and Apple handles the refund. We cannot process it for you, though we can tell you what to ask for. Everything else on this page you take up with us directly.

Clause 2

The 10 day trial, which is why this page is rarely needed

No card, nothing to refund

Every new account gets 10 days of Pro with 300 resources and no card details. When the 10 days end you are moved to the free plan rather than charged. Your library stays with you. Nobody is ever locked out of work they have already made.

So by the time you pay us anything, you have made real lessons with the real product for a fortnight. We would rather you spent that fortnight deciding than spent it reading this.

Clause 3

Monthly plans

Full refund within 14 days

Write to us within 14 days of any monthly payment and we refund that payment in full. We do not count what you used, we do not ask you to justify it, and we do not offer you three alternatives first. One short message is enough.

After 14 days a monthly payment is not automatically refundable, because you have had most of the month. Cancel instead and you keep the plan until the period you have paid for runs out. Nothing further is taken.

On the half price first month. If you took Pro at £3.99 for your first month, that is what we refund. The discount is a one off, so if you come back later the price is the standard one.

Clause 4

Yearly plans

Full refund within 14 days

Within 14 days of a yearly payment we refund it in full. We do not count what you used first. A yearly plan is a bigger commitment than a monthly one, so if anything the 14 days matter more here, and we are not going to hand them back to you with a deduction attached.

The lessons you made during those 14 days stay in your library. We are not going to take work off a teacher because they asked for their money back.

After the first 14 days

A yearly plan is not refundable by default once the 14 days have passed, because the low yearly price is the whole reason it is low. But teaching is not a stable job to hold, and we would rather be asked than have you write us off:

  • You are leaving teaching, or taking long term leave, and write to tell us
  • Your school or trust has bought a licence that now covers you
  • You have been made redundant

In those cases write to us and we will refund the whole months you have not used. We do not ask for evidence. We are aware that trusting people here costs us a little money and we have decided we would rather pay it.

Clause 5

Top ups

Refundable while unspent

A top up is refundable in full for 14 days as long as you have not spent any of it. Once you start spending a top up it is not refundable, because what you bought has been delivered and is sitting in your library.

Top up resources do not expire and do not disappear at the end of the month. They sit underneath your monthly allowance and are only touched once the allowance is gone. If you cancel your plan, any unspent top up resources stay on your account and still work.

One thing worth knowing before you top up. Picture slideshows cost considerably more than text resources, so a top up bought for picture work goes faster than the same top up spent on worksheets. The counter in your dashboard shows both separately, and we would rather you saw that before you bought than after.

Clause 6

A renewal you did not mean to pay

Full refund within 30 days

If your plan renewed and you had meant to cancel, tell us within 30 days of that payment and we refund it in full, provided you have not used the plan since it was taken. There is nothing to explain and no form to fill in.

We also do not think it is your job to remember. We email you before a yearly plan renews and before any price changes. If one of those emails did not arrive, that is on us, and clause 7 applies.

Clause 7

When the fault is ours

Always refunded, no time limit

Some things are not a change of mind, they are a mistake, and the 14 and 30 day limits on this page do not apply to them:

  • You were charged twice for the same period
  • You were charged after cancelling
  • You were charged a price you had not agreed to
  • Jooma was unavailable or broken for a meaningful stretch of the period you paid for
  • Resources were deducted for something that failed to generate, which we return to your balance as well

Write to us and we will fix it. If we got it wrong we will say so plainly rather than calling it a goodwill gesture.

Clause 8

Schools, trusts and other organisations

Separate terms apply

Buying for a school, a trust or a local authority is a business purchase, so consumer cancellation rights do not apply and your written agreement with us governs instead. As a default, where that agreement says nothing else:

  • Full refund within 30 days of the invoice date if no staff accounts have been activated
  • After that, a refund of the whole months not yet used, at the per seat rate you agreed
  • Onboarding and training fees are not refundable once the onboarding has been delivered. If it has been booked but not delivered, it is refunded in full
  • Seats removed partway through a term are not refunded individually. They are freed for someone else to use

If a teacher at your school is already paying for Jooma personally when you buy a licence, tell us and we will refund the unused part of their personal subscription. They should not be paying twice for a decision made above them.

Clause 9

What we do not refund

The short list
  • Top ups you have started spending, under clause 5. The lessons they produced stay in your library either way
  • Periods already served, beyond the windows set out above
  • Accounts closed by us for serious misuse, such as sharing one login across a staff room or reselling what Jooma produces

That is the whole list. If something has gone wrong and it is not on this page, write to us anyway. A policy cannot anticipate every situation and we would rather hear about it than have you assume the answer is no.

How to ask, and how long it takes

One email. No form, no phone queue, no retention script. A person reads it and answers within two working days, and once approved the money goes back to the card you paid with, which takes five to ten working days to appear depending on your bank.

You do not have to tell us why. If you want to, we read every one of those, and it is the most useful post we get.
