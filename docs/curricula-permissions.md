# Curricula: permissions to ask for (send together after the last stage)

Owner's rule (2026-11-24): no permission email is sent during the stages. Every curriculum whose statement text Jooma cannot quote without permission is listed here, and all requests go out together at the end. Teachers on these curricula still get the full profile (year names, terms, exams); only the "Align to curriculum" statement list waits.

When a reply comes back: record it here (date, who, wording, any fee), then for a yes add it to `app/lib/standards/permissions.ts` and build the statements as in `docs/curricula-gaps-plan.md` section 4. A fee comes back to the owner first.

## The email

Use the draft in `docs/curricula-gaps-plan.md` section 7, filling in the curriculum, the documents and the body. Exceptions:

- **Wales:** use the Wales draft in `docs/uk-curricula-build.md` section 10.
- **NGSS:** two asks, with their own conditions (see `app/lib/standards/permissions.ts`): WestEd for the trademark (send samples to nextgenscience@wested.org; 4 to 6 weeks) and the National Academies Press for the text.
- **South Africa:** ask DBE to waive the non-commercial condition of its Creative Commons licence.
- **Philippines:** ask DepEd for the prior approval Republic Act 8293 s.176 requires to use a government work for profit.
- **British Columbia:** use the province's Copyright Permission Request Form, not email.
