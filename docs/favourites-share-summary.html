Favourites and Share Links

Jooma, 20 Nov 2026. Built and tested on staging. Database change on production. Code not committed yet.

Your 4 picks

What share link opens Public view page

Anyone with link sees read only copy. No sign in. Has "Make your own" button. Jooma teachers can save a copy. Teacher can turn link off.

Which tools can share Classroom resources only

Slides, worksheets, comprehension, quizzes, homework, plans and more. Reports, pupil profiles, behaviour, SEND, letters and staff docs stay colleague only. No pupil name in WhatsApp group.

Where stars live Account + sidebar

Stars save to teacher account. Same at school and home. Sidebar "Pinned" now "Favourites". Old pins move over. Teacher can unstar defaults.

What goes A to Z Categories and filter chips

Sections and filter buttons go A to Z. Tools inside each section keep their order. Most used first.

What got made

  • Favourites: star icon on each tool. Favourites on top. Slides, Worksheets, Comprehension starred for all.
  • A to Z: Assessment, Behaviour and SEND, Classroom resources, Communication, Leadership, Planning, Slides.
  • Share: Library menu says "Share". Dialog has Colleagues and Link. Link gives WhatsApp, Viber, Messenger, Instagram, Copy link.
  • Public page: /share/<token>. No teacher name. Hidden from Google. Nice preview card in chat apps.

Two app quirks

  • Instagram: no web share link exists. Phone uses share sheet. Computer copies link, opens Instagram inbox.
  • Messenger on computer: copies link, opens messenger.com. Direct send needs Facebook App ID in NEXT_PUBLIC_FACEBOOK_APP_ID.
