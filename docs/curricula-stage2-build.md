Jooma is a paid product, so a non-commercial permission is a no.

| Country | Body | What we found | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| South Africa | Department of Basic Education | DBE's own Creative Commons notice: reuse with attribution, **non-commercial only**; commercial use (a fee charged by a for-profit) needs the Department's permission, which it says it can give | Closed: ask for a commercial waiver |
