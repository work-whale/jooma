Where does rain come from?

Original text, written for this lesson

The sun warms the sea. Tiny bits of water float up into the sky. This is called evaporation.

High up, the air is cold. The water cools and makes clouds. When the drops get heavy, they fall as rain.

The rain runs into rivers. The rivers carry it back to the sea, and it all starts again.

Questions
  1. What warms the sea?
  2. What is it called when water goes up into the sky?
  3. Why do the drops fall?
  4. Draw the journey water takes, from sea to rain to river.

Questions

Before you start.

What actually is Jooma?

Thirty five tools that make the things teachers make. You pick a tool, answer a few questions such as year group, subject and topic, and it hands you a finished resource you can edit, print or present. There is no prompt writing and nothing to learn.

What is a credit?

How we price the work. Every tool shows what it costs before you press the button, and bigger jobs such as a full teaching deck cost more than a single worksheet. Pro gives you 2,000 credits a month and you can top up any time. Editing or refining something you have already made never costs anything.

Is it aligned to the UK curriculum?

Yes. Every tool is written around the national curriculum for England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. You choose your curriculum once and it applies everywhere, so year group and key stage are built in rather than bolted on.

Who is Jo?

Jo is the assistant built into Jooma. Tell it what you are teaching in plain English and it opens the right tool with the details already filled in. It asks a question back if something is unclear, so you are never guessing what it will produce.

Can I share resources with colleagues?

Yes. Add colleagues by name, username or email, then share any resource straight to their library. They get their own copy to edit, and yours stays untouched. Most schools end up with a shared bank of resources built by the people who teach there.

Can I edit what it makes?

All of it. Change the wording, the reading age, the difficulty or a single question. Everything saves to your library so you can pick it up next year.

Is pupil data safe?

Jooma never asks for pupil names or personal data and you should not enter any. Everything you make belongs to you and is stored in the UK.

What if my school wants it for everyone?

Schools and trusts get pooled credits, an admin dashboard, usage reporting and one invoice, starting at £4.25 per teacher and falling with size. Get in touch and we will put a quote together.
